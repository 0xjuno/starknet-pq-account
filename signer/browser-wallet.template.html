PQ account signer

This Sepolia account started as a plain OpenZeppelin account and was migrated in place. Every transaction now needs a STARK-curve ECDSA signature and a Falcon-512 signature. Both keys come from one 24-word mnemonic.

Keys are derived in this tab and never leave it. The words are not saved.

01

Restore keys from the mnemonic

The demo account's words are public on purpose (Sepolia, topped up now and then). Don't paste a real wallet's words here or on any other page.

03

Passkey (optional)

Lock the restored keys with a passkey (Face ID, Windows Hello, security key) and unlock with it next time instead of typing the words. The seed is encrypted with the passkey's PRF output and kept in this browser. The words stay the backup.

No passkey-locked keys on this device.

Signature, 62 felts

r, s: ECDSA (2) s2 packed: Falcon-512 (29) salt (2) s2 × h hint (29)

__validate__

  • ECDSA over the tx hash, against the stored STARK key
  • Falcon-512 over the same hash, by library call to the verifier class (OpenZeppelin cairo-pq-verifiers, SHAKE-256)
  • Both have to pass

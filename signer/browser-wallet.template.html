How it works

What the account checks

Every transaction carries 62 felts. Both halves are checked against the same transaction hash.

ECDSA r, s (2) Falcon-512 s2 (29) Salt (2) s2 × h hint (29)
  • 1
    ECDSA over the transaction hash
    against the account's stored STARK key
  • 2
    Falcon-512 over the same hash
    by library call to the account's verifier class: OpenZeppelin cairo-pq-verifiers, or the Provably Fast small verifier
  • 3
    Both have to pass
    a valid ECDSA signature alone is rejected in __validate__
